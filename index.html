LauncherWidget/Counter

The worked sample: AndroidX's MyWidget counter rebuilt from this sheet's WidgetSurface and WidgetButton, drawn at 3x2 and resized across the grid as a launcher would.
×

LauncherWidget/Grid

The launcher grid sizes a widget design is authored in — 1x1 to 5x2 — at Android's documented portrait cell size, (73n − 16) × (118m − 16) dp.

WidgetSurface

The widget background: fills the cell, rounds it to Android 12's 16dp widget radius and pads its content by 12dp.
